Art Is a Journey. Mine Brought Me Back to My Passion.

I’m the charcoal artist and art instructor behind Sanware Ki Artshala. My journey began with studying fashion designing. After marriage, life took me in different directions, and years later I returned to art.

Today I create realistic charcoal portraits and teach charcoal art and painting to children and adults.

“Sometimes, the passions we return to become the most meaningful part of our journey.”
